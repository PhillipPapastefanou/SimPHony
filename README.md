# The SimPHony model 

The **Sim**ple **P**lant **H**ydraulics **on**l**y** model is standalone modelling tool that describes the water flow throught a plant via roots, stem and leaves 
based on the fundamental laws of water transport.
The name simphony is an acronym for symphony, which generally refers to a large-scale orchestral composition
with a complex structure, typically consisting of four movements. Despite its simplicity the SimPHony model exhibits complex behavior and its
major parameters need to (harmonically) play together to mimic realistic plant water flows and contents. Furthermore, the SimPHony model development was exclusively accompanied by classical symphonies by renowned composers such as Bach and Beethoven.
To maintain the model's integrity and ensure its continued evolution, this musical tradition will be upheld in future development endeavors.
